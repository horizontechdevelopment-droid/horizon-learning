During this module you learned:

- Why Kali Linux includes so many tools.
- What Nmap is designed to do.
- What Wireshark is designed to do.
- Why permission always comes before testing.
- How to begin documenting your own network.

Most importantly...

You learned that cybersecurity tools help us **observe and understand** systems—not magically solve problems.
